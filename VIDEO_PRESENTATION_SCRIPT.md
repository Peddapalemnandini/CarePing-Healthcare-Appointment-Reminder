# CarePing — 2 to 3 Minute Video Script

## 0:00–0:20 — Introduction
“Hello, my project is CarePing, a simple healthcare appointment reminder and scheduling prototype. The aim is to make it easier to organize appointments and quickly identify visits coming up today or over the next seven days.”

## 0:20–0:45 — Tools and why
“I used Python and Streamlit because they let me create a working web interface quickly without paid tools or a complex front end. I used an AI assistant during development to plan the features, generate a first version, review edge cases, and improve the documentation. The app itself uses Python logic and does not call an AI service at runtime.”

## 0:45–1:15 — Prompt engineering
“I started with a prompt describing the assessment, the one-day time limit, and the requirement to use free tools. I then refined the prompt to specify the fields, appointment counts, sample data, CSV export, and input validation. In follow-up prompts, I focused on empty states, sorting, record removal, privacy, and making the limitations clear. I kept the prompts and refinement notes in the project documentation.”

## 1:15–2:20 — Live demo
“Here is the CarePing dashboard. I will load fictional sample appointments so we can see the app immediately. The summary shows the total appointments, how many are today, and how many fall within the next seven days. In the appointment list, records are sorted by date and time, and upcoming visits are described as today, tomorrow, or in a number of days. I can add a new fictional appointment, remove a selected record, and export the list as a CSV file.”

## 2:20–2:45 — Challenge, solution, and limitations
“One challenge was making the project useful without building a full healthcare platform. I kept the scope to scheduling, clear upcoming-appointment information, and export. I also added validation and a privacy note. This is an educational prototype: it does not send actual SMS or email reminders and should not be used with real patient information.”

## 2:45–3:00 — Conclusion
“This project helped me practice using AI prompts to plan, build, refine, and document a small application. Thank you for watching.”

## Recording checklist
- Show the app running locally.
- Demonstrate at least one feature live.
- Keep the video at least two minutes.
- Mention the actual AI tool(s) you used.
- Show prompt documentation or explain the prompt revisions.
- Be honest about what is implemented and what is only a future improvement.
